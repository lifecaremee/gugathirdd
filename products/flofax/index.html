Flofax

Antibacterial solution for poultry

Composition

Each 100ml contains:

Ofloxacin 10gm
Core Mechanism of Action
  • Absorption & Bioavailability: Ofloxacin is rapidly and effectively absorbed in poultry (specially in broilers), showing very high oral bioavailability (approximately 95-98%) and reaching peak plasma concentrations quickly.
  • Distribution: It distributes widely into body tissues and fluids, making it effective against systemic and localized infections, such as E. coli.
  • Elimination: It is eliminated faster in broiler chickens compared to any old fluoroquinolones, with a standard recommended dosage typically around 10mg/kg body weight every 24 hours.
How Ofloxacin Works

Ofloxacin is a powerful fluoroquinolone antibiotic that exhibits potent bactericidal activity against Escherichia coli by targeting DNA gyrase and Topoisomerase IV to halt DNA replication. Its dual action mechanism targets essential enzymes of both Gram-negative and Gram-positive bacteria.

  • Shields against E. coli infections
  • Enhances better production

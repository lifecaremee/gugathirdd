Vitality Enhancer

INDICATION:

    • IBD ( Infectious Bursal Disease ), EDS ( Egg Drop Syndrome ), HPS ( Hydro Pericardium Syndrome ) and IBH ( Inclusion Body Hepatitis )
    • High mortality rate in broiler at any age which can also be diagnosed by chicken anaemia

    BENEFITS:

    • MLC 360 supports bird to fight against viral disease such as HPS, IBD, IBH and EDS
    • MLC 360 continuous use during viral attack keeps the bird's immunity boosted to fight by limiting the load
    • MLC 360 not only reduces the mortality but also has anti oxidant components in it to stimulate immunity cells
    • MLC 360 cover capsid and non-capsid virus at every stage (attachment, penetration, uncoating, replication, maturation and release )
    • Compensate lack of appetite
    • Protection against free radicals
Packaging

25 Liters

Dosage
  • Drinking Water: 1 ml per 2-3 Liters of Water
